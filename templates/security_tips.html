🔐 CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS

Stay Safe From Phishing

Phishing attacks attempt to trick users into revealing sensitive information. Follow these security practices to recognize suspicious emails and protect your digital identity.

🔗

Check Suspicious Links

Before clicking a link, check where it leads. Be careful with shortened URLs, unfamiliar domains, and links asking for sensitive information.

📧

Verify the Sender

Carefully examine the sender's email address. Attackers may use addresses that look similar to legitimate organizations.

⚠️

Watch for Urgent Messages

Be cautious when an email creates panic or pressure by demanding immediate payment, verification, login, or account recovery.

🔑

Protect Your Passwords

Never share passwords or one-time verification codes through unexpected emails. Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.

🏦

Verify Financial Requests

If an email asks for banking information, payment, or account verification, independently contact the organization using its official website or known contact details.

📎

Be Careful With Attachments

Avoid opening unexpected attachments. Malicious files can contain software designed to compromise your device or information.

🛡️

Keep Software Updated

Keep your operating system, browser, security software, and applications updated to reduce exposure to known vulnerabilities.

🤖

Use AI Email Analysis

When an email looks suspicious, use the AI Email Analyzer in this system to examine the message and receive a phishing prediction.

🚨 Never Trust Pressure Alone

Phishing messages often use urgency, fear, rewards, threats, or unexpected requests to make people act quickly. Stop, verify the information independently, and avoid sharing sensitive information until you are confident that the request is legitimate.

✅ Quick Security Checklist

✓ Did I recognize the sender?
✓ Does the email address look legitimate?
✓ Is the message creating unnecessary urgency?
✓ Did I verify the link before clicking?
✓ Is the attachment expected?
✓ Am I being asked for passwords, OTPs, or financial information?
✓ Should I analyze this email before taking action?
